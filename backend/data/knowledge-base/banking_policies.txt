BANKING CUSTOMER SUPPORT POLICIES

CARD PAYMENT FAILED

If a card payment fails but the customer sees a pending amount:
- The amount may be a temporary authorization hold.
- The hold may automatically disappear after the merchant or bank completes processing.
- The customer should not assume that a permanent debit has occurred until the transaction status is confirmed.

If the transaction is confirmed as completed but the customer did not receive the expected service or product:
- The customer may need to contact the merchant first.
- A dispute may be initiated according to the bank's dispute policy.

CARD PAYMENT REVERSED

A reversed card payment means the transaction was initially authorized but subsequently reversed.
The customer should wait for the reversal to reflect in the account.
If the reversal does not appear within the expected processing period, the customer should contact customer support.

UNKNOWN TRANSACTION

If a customer reports an unknown transaction:
- Do not assume that the transaction is fraudulent.
- Verify the transaction details.
- Ask the customer to confirm whether they recognize the merchant.
- If fraud is suspected, escalate the case according to the bank's fraud procedure.

ACCOUNT SECURITY

Never ask customers to provide:
- Full password
- PIN
- One-time password (OTP)
- Full card number
- CVV

The AI agent must never request or expose these sensitive credentials.

HUMAN ESCALATION

Escalate to a human support representative when:
- The customer's issue requires a banking action that the available tools cannot perform.
- The customer reports suspected fraud.
- The agent has insufficient information to provide a reliable answer.
- The customer explicitly requests human assistance.
